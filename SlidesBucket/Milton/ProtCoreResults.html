Differential abundance (DA) analysis

The following slides (→) display the results of the differential abundance analyses performed for each of the comparisons of interest using the limma R package. Following limma testing, p-values are retained only when the condition associated with higher expression (as determined by the sign of the logFC) has a proportion of imputed values lower than the specified threshold, controlling for imputation artefacts. Reported FDRs are calculated using the Benjamini-Hochberg correction, applied only to proteins that meet the logFC threshold.

 

In the resulting volcano plots, use the legend to isolate data types, zoom and pan to get a better look, and investigate possible proteins of interest by clicking the points to follow the associated UniProt link.

Clustering of DA proteins

Clustering the intensities of all previously defined DA proteins can help us identify cohorts of proteins that show similar intensity profiles under certain conditions, and is especially useful when conditions may be considered more or less similar to one another in some aspect and similar patterns of protein expression reflect a common effect. The following slides (→) show how the proteins clustered based on intensities and details for each cluster.

 

For each cluster, we can perform an overrepresentation analysis to identify functional enrichments. Use the down arrows (↓) to explore the overrepresentation analyses for each cluster. Here, we consider the GO Biological Process  and Reactome Pathways 2024 gene set libraries.
