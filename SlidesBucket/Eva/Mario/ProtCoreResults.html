A note on navigating the data

The presentation follows a horizontal structure (→) for main content. Vertical slides (↓) are used to explore deeper details for specific datasets or provide alternate perspectives on the same data. Keep an eye out for these vertical branches during navigation. You can press the ESC key to collapse the slide deck and navigate quickly.

 

Many of the plots are interactive — you can zoom and pan intuitively by clicking and dragging to explore the data in more detail.

Gene Set Enrichment Analysis

We can also perform a gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA). This differs from an ORA because here we consider all proteins, rather than a subset that meet specific criteria, such as logFC. This may identify more subtle patterns of protein co-expression.
